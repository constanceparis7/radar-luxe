How to attend the Monaco Grand Prix

Tickets are sold only through the official ticket office of the Automobile Club de Monaco, monaco-grandprix.com, or at its counter at 44 rue Grimaldi. The 2027 edition is announced from Thursday 3 to Sunday 6 June, and sales open in the coming weeks: the best grandstands go as soon as sales open.

There is only one official ticket office: monaco-grandprix.com, the website of the Automobile Club de Monaco, or its counter at 44 rue Grimaldi in Monaco (tel. 00 377 93 15 26 24, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm). The 2027 edition is announced from Thursday 3 to Sunday 6 June, with the race on Sunday, the dates still subject to the FIA's validation of the F1 calendar in autumn 2026. Sales for 2027 open in the coming weeks: signing up for the ACM newsletter gets you notified, and the best grandstands and terraces go as soon as sales open. The pricing structure works as follows: Thursday at a reduced rate (free for ages 6 to 15), 2-day (Saturday and Sunday) and 3-day (Friday through Sunday) packages with a 10% discount on seated places, children under 6 free on a lap. As for categories: GOLD grandstands (sectors K1, K2, K3, K6, L, B, V) and SILVER, VIP Belvédère and Loges packages with lounge and catering (hospitalite@acm.mc), F1 Paddock Club distributed via formula1.com. The 2027 prices are not yet published: the 2026 price list serves as the reference at opening. There is no free entry; the only non-paying route is professional press accreditation, through the ACM or the FIA, subject to strict conditions.
