When to book the LVMH Journées Particulières

Reservations open in the course of September 2026 on the official website lvmh.com, for the October 16 to 18, 2026 edition; no precise opening date has been published. Sign up now via the site's email alert form to be notified, and book your slot the moment reservations open.

The official website announces only that reservations will open at some point in September 2026, with no precise day, for the edition running from Friday, October 16 to Sunday, October 18, 2026. As of August 29, 2026, roughly 7 weeks before the event, ticketing by venue and time slot had not yet opened: none of the venue pages consulted showed a booking button, only practical information. In the meantime, the site offers an email alert sign-up form: this is the way to be notified when the "Carnet d'expériences" opens, and to book your slot right away. Booking is free, with no code or referral required, subject to available places, and each venue applies its own age and access conditions. As for the traps: there is no paid ticket, no VIP access for sale, and no official resale; any route other than lvmh.com should therefore be ruled out. On Place Vendôme, the "Diadèmes de Rêve" visit at Chaumet (12 Place Vendôme) and the opening of the Repossi boutique (6 Place Vendôme) follow the same calendar: registrations opening in the course of September 2026 via the Journées Particulières website, by selecting the desired experience as soon as it is published.
