How much does a ticket to the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe cost

From €10 in the Village de l'Arc and €20 for the general enclosure, on the official France Galop ticketing site. The other enclosure categories range from €60 (Pelouse de l'Arrivée Or) to €300 (Tribune de l'Arrivée Or), and hospitality packages from €239 to €827. Pricing is dynamic: prices climb as October 3 and 4, 2026 approach.

The Qatar Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe is run on October 3 and 4, 2026 at the ParisLongchamp racecourse, and everything is booked through the official France Galop ticketing site. Pricing is dynamic: the amounts announced are starting prices, which climb as the date approaches. Inside the racecourse, expect from €10 in the Village de l'Arc, from €20 for the general enclosure, from €60 in the Pelouse de l'Arrivée Or, from €80 at the Sommet de l'Arc, from €110 at the Bistro Triomphe Hespéride and from €300 in the Tribune de l'Arrivée Or.

Hospitality packages, with lunch and a view of the finish line, run as follows: Brasserie ParisLongchamp €239, Prestige €299, Prestige Brasserie €467, Prestige Auberge €599, the Ribot package €659 and Prestige Cascade €827. Third-party providers approved by France Galop also offer them, including Racing Breaks for the British market. The trap is the calendar: the Prestige Cascade hospitality and the Tribune de l'Arrivée Or, the best-placed options, are the first to sell out. Booking early therefore means both securing a seat and paying less. One last detail noted on our event page: strict dress, jacket and tie, is required.
