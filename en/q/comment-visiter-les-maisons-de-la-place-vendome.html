How to visit the Maisons of Place Vendôme

The Maisons of Place Vendôme open their doors during LVMH's Les Journées Particulières, October 16 to 18, 2026: Chaumet at No. 12, Repossi at No. 6, plus Bvlgari and Fred on the same square and on Rue de la Paix. Access is entirely free, with online booking via lvmh.com; reservations open during September 2026.

The access window is Les Journées Particulières, the LVMH event whose 6th edition, on the theme "Aux Racines du Rêve," opens 69 sites of 46 Maisons in 12 countries, from Friday, October 16 to Sunday, October 18, 2026. In Paris, the private mansions of Place Vendôme take part: Chaumet at No. 12, Repossi at No. 6, plus Bvlgari and Fred on the same square and on Rue de la Paix. At Chaumet, the "Diadèmes de Rêve" visit is free, accessible to visitors with reduced mobility, from age 6; at Repossi, the historic boutique at 6 Place Vendôme (its headquarters since 1986, marking 40 years in 2026) welcomes visitors from age 8, accompanied.

Everything is free and everything runs through a single channel: the official site lvmh.com/les-journees-particulieres-2026 and its catalogue of experiences, where you book a time slot, venue by venue, subject to availability. There is no paid ticket, no purchasable VIP access, and no official resale. Reservations open during September 2026; in the meantime, the site offers an email alert form to be notified as soon as they open. Institutional contact: lesjourneesparticulieres@lvmh.com, +33 (0)1 44 13 22 22.
