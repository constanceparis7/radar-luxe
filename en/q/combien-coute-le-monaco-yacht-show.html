How much does entry to the Monaco Yacht Show cost

Entry to the 2026 Monaco Yacht Show costs €700 including tax for one day, Thursday through Saturday, €1,300 for two days, and €2,070 for three days. Wednesday, September 23, a day reserved for guests and private clients, is €400. Tickets are sold exclusively online, with no box office on site.

The show runs from September 23 to 26, 2026 at Port Hercule. The official price list, tax included, has two regimes. Wednesday, September 23, the opening day reserved for guests and private clients with Dockside access, is €400. Thursday through Saturday, the one-day pass costs €700, the two-day pass €1,300, and the three-day pass €2,070.

Two points to watch. First, sales are exclusively online: there is no box office on site and nothing is sold on the quay. Second, the most sought-after categories sell out before summer; buy your pass as soon as ticketing opens. The organizer, Informa Markets, also offers an official VIP program and a Press Pass, both on request. Private parties orbit the show as well, such as My Yacht Group's aboard a superyacht moored in Port Hercule: access by invitation with mandatory RSVP, price not published, evening dress or long gown required. Write from late summer, introducing yourself (profile, company, reason for the request), as the list closes before the show opens and a late request is no longer processed.
