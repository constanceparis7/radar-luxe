amfAR Gala Cannes 2027 (rolling season)

May 2027 during the Cannes Film Festival — the 2026 edition (21 May) has already taken place; 2027 date estimated, to be confirmed by amfAR · Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc, Cap d'Antibes · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 82/100

The most glamorous charity gala on the Riviera, set beside the gardens of the Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc: a prestige dinner, a fashion show, a live auction and a concert by a superstar (Robbie Williams, Zara Larsson and PinkPantheress were on the 2026 line-up). A genuine, published route in via the purchase of a seat or a table from amfAR.

Line-up : The 2027 line-up has not yet been announced (2026 reference: Robbie Williams, Zara Larsson, PinkPantheress)

How to get in

amfAR (The Foundation for AIDS Research)

Purchase of a seat or a table via the official amfAR ticketing (events.amfar.org) or by contacting the amfAR Cannes office

The turnkey stay

The morning after the amfAR Gala, extend the magic of the Cap in its most intimate setting: fragrant pine groves, turquoise coves and Belle Époque villas, just minutes from the Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc. Between waterfront palace hotels, starred tables and wellness rituals facing the Mediterranean, this stay orchestrates Riviera art de vivre in its rarest form. Every moment prolongs the splendour of the evening, from a spa awakening to a bouillabaisse dinner at sunset.

Where to stay

  • Cap d'Antibes Beach Hotel — A five-star Relais & Châteaux with its feet in the sand — the only hotel on the Cap with a private beach of fine sand, 35 rooms and suites and a starred table facing the Mediterranean, just minutes from the Eden-Roc.
  • Hôtel Belles Rives — An Art Déco palace hotel in Juan-les-Pins, born in 1929 in the former villa of F. Scott Fitzgerald; private jetty, legendary bar and the starred restaurant La Passagère overlooking the bay.

Where to dine

  • Maison de Bacon — An institution of Cap d'Antibes since 1948, the absolute benchmark for bouillabaisse and Mediterranean fish grilled over a wood fire, with a terrace open to the sea and the Fort Carré.
  • Le Figuier de Saint-Esprit — One Michelin star in old Antibes, the exceptional Provençal cuisine of chef Christian Morisset served in a garden-patio beneath a hundred-year-old fig tree.

What to experience

  • Dior Spa Eden-Roc — Signature spa within the Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc — the very setting of the Gala: treatment suites, hydrotherapy and open-air cabins for a recovery ritual facing the pines and the sea.
  • Plage Keller, Restaurant Le César — The legendary private beach of the Baie de la Garoupe since 1931: wooden pontoons over crystal-clear water, a Mediterranean lunch and idle hours where Picasso and Chaplin once bathed.
