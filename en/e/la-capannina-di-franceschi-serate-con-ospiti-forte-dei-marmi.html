The most legendary of Italian clubs, opened in 1929, a jewel of Versilia glamour. Dinner followed by dancing and guest concerts (Achille Lauro, Elettra Lamborghini in recent seasons), revival evenings, Sapori di Mare: an institution where Italian high society mingles. Table reservation essential for the big nights.

Line-up : Guests and artists announced according to the seasonal calendar, released as summer unfolds (in the past: Achille Lauro, Elettra Lamborghini) — no 2026 line-up confirmed to date.

How to get in

La Capannina di Franceschi

Table / apericena reservations via the official website (Prenota page) or by telephone; concert tickets via liveticket.it/lacapanninadifranceschi.

The turnkey stay

Stepping out of La Capannina di Franceschi, Versilia's nocturnal institution since 1929, the night carries on amid the most refined setting on the Tuscan coast: pine groves, Belle Époque villas and fine-sand beaches facing the Apuan Alps. Nights in a beachfront palace hotel, Michelin-starred seafood lunches, lazing in a private cabana at the Bocelli family's beach and an escapade through the marble quarries that gave Forte dei Marmi its name. Italian elegance, from the last cocktail at dawn to the first plunge into the sea.

Where to stay

  • Principe Forte dei Marmi — A five-star palace hotel, member of The Leading Hotels of the World, on the seafront along Viale Morin, just minutes from La Capannina. Egoista Spa, the Michelin-starred panoramic restaurant Lux Lucis, the 67 Sky Lounge rooftop and a private beach.
  • Hotel Byron — A five-star Relais & Châteaux villa in the heart of Forte dei Marmi, with a heated seawater pool and hushed, discreet service. Home to the Michelin-starred restaurant La Magnolia.

Where to dine

  • Ristorante Lorenzo — A Michelin-starred institution in the centre of Forte dei Marmi, famed for its catch from the Tyrrhenian Sea, its mayonnaise prepared tableside and one of the finest cellars in Versilia.
  • Ristorante Bistrot — The Michelin-starred table of chef Andrea Mattei, on the seafront along Viale Franceschi. Tyrrhenian fish cooked in a wood-fired oven, a true gastronomic spirit and an exceptional wine list.

What to experience

  • Bagno Alpemare, Beach Club des Bocelli — An exceptional private beach created by the Bocelli family: 48 tented cabanas, haute-couture service and the Alpemare restaurant facing the Apuan Alps.
  • Cave di Marmo Tours, carrières de marbre de Carrare — A private 4x4 tour into the working quarries of Carrara (Torano, Miseglia, Colonnata), a visit to the spectacular Marble Cathedral and a tasting of lardo di Colonnata, about 30 min from Forte dei Marmi.
