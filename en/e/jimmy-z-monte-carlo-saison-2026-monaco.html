Jimmy'z Monte-Carlo (2026 season)

Summer 2026 season (Thu-Sat in July-August; closing with Bob Sinclar X COYA Night on 25 September) · Jimmy'z Monte-Carlo, Sporting Monte-Carlo · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 81/100

Monte-Carlo's legendary open-air club, a lush garden thrown open to the night and the Principality's benchmark for deep house and electro. The 2026 line-ups feature international headliners, rapper Quavo, house icon Bob Sinclar closing the season, alongside signature « Made by Jimmy'z » nights. Table bookings available, public access documented.

Line-up : Bob Sinclar (closing 25 Sept.), Quavo, Carlita, Alex Wann

How to get in

Monte-Carlo Société des Bains de Mer

Table reservation / bottle service via the official SevenRooms platform (eu.sevenrooms.com/reservations/jimmyzmontecarlo) or the SBM line

The turnkey stay

Extend the magic of Jimmy'z nights without ever leaving the Larvotto peninsula: waterfront palace hotels, two Michelin-starred tables signed by Marcel Ravin and the largest seawater spa on the Riviera, all a few steps from the Sporting. A Monégasque art de vivre where the exceptional evening carries on until daybreak, between turquoise lagoon, private beach and wellness rituals facing the open blue.

Where to stay

  • Monte-Carlo Bay Hotel & Resort — A resort set on a 4-hectare peninsula, adjoining the Sporting and Jimmy'z: a turquoise sandy-bottomed lagoon, the Spa Cinq Mondes and the 2-starred Blue Bay table. The closest address for walking home after the party.
  • Le Méridien Beach Plaza — The only hotel in Monaco with its own private beach, a Larvotto landmark on Avenue Princesse Grace, a few minutes' walk from the Sporting Monte-Carlo and Jimmy'z.

Where to dine

  • Blue Bay Marcel Ravin — Two Michelin stars at the Monte-Carlo Bay: the Creole-Mediterranean repertoire of Martinique-born chef Marcel Ravin, house organic kitchen garden and Mr Goodfish-labelled fish. A stone's throw from Jimmy'z.
  • Elsa, Monte-Carlo Beach — A Michelin-starred table and the world's first fully organic starred restaurant, Mediterranean cuisine facing the sea on the legendary Monte-Carlo Beach.

What to experience

  • Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo — A 6,600 m² seawater spa devoted to wellbeing: a 25 m heated seawater pool, hammam, sauna, sentorium and La Prairie and Phytomer treatments facing the Mediterranean. Ideal for post-party recovery.
  • Lagon & plage du Monte-Carlo Bay — A day of relaxation on the Monte-Carlo Bay's turquoise sandy-bottomed lagoon: solariums, cabanas and waterfront bathing, literally adjoining Jimmy'z and the Sporting.
