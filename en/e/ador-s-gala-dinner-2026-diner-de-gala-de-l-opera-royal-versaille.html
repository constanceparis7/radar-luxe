ADOR's Gala Dinner 2026, Opéra Royal Gala Dinner

Saturday 3 October 2026, from 16:30 · Opéra Royal, Château de Versailles · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 80/100

ADOR's 9th charity gala dinner in aid of the Opéra Royal: champagne reception (Salle des Croisades), gala concert 'From Salzburg to Naples: lyric jewels of Mozart, Rossini, Donizetti', cocktails in the Salon d'Hercule, a musical interlude in the Chapelle Royale and a promenade through the Grands Appartements, grand supper in the Galerie des Batailles, then fireworks from the Galerie des Glaces. A patronage dinner of the highest order, with individual seats or tables of 10, open to donors (patronage tax deduction).

Line-up : Gwendoline Blondeel (soprano) and soloists of the Académie de l'Opéra Royal; Choir and Orchestra of the Opéra Royal under the baton of Gaétan Jarry (Mozart / Rossini / Donizetti programme)

How to get in

ADOR, Les Amis de l'Opéra Royal de Versailles

Bienfaiteur seat €1,400 or Grand Bienfaiteur €1,600; Grand Bienfaiteur package with a night at the Waldorf Astoria Trianon Palace Versailles €3,700 (for two); Prestige table €16,000 or Premium €20,000 (10 seats). Patronage tax deduction. Online booking closed: contact the ADOR office (official phone/email) or the box office on 01 30 83 78 89 (Mon-Fri 11:00-18:00), 3 bis rue des Réservoirs, 78000 Versailles.

The turnkey stay

Extend the magic of the Opéra Royal by staying just steps from the Château's gates. Between a palace nestled within the very Estate of Versailles and the Belle Époque elegance of the Trianon Palace, your stay embraces the French art of living: a Michelin-starred dinner, a Guerlain spa and an early-morning boat outing on the Grand Canal. Two days suspended in the world of the Sun King, from the gala to the last shimmer of the grounds.

Where to stay

  • Airelles Château de Versailles, Le Grand Contrôle — The only 5-star palace set within the grounds of the Estate of Versailles (11 rooms and 2 suites, an Alain Ducasse restaurant, an Airelles spa), a few minutes' walk from the Opéra Royal.
  • Waldorf Astoria Versailles - Trianon Palace — A 1910 Belle Époque palace bordering the palace park, with a 2,800 m² Guerlain spa and a Gordon Ramsay restaurant, less than 10 minutes from the Château.

Where to dine

  • La Table du 11 — The Michelin-starred table of chef Jean-Baptiste Lavergne-Morazzani, Cour des Senteurs, a stone's throw from the Château: seasonal cuisine, organic produce and a family kitchen garden.
  • Gordon Ramsay au Trianon — A 1-Michelin-star restaurant within the Trianon Palace, 5- or 7-course menus signed by Gordon Ramsay, overlooking the park of Versailles.

What to experience

  • Spa Guerlain, Trianon Palace — 2,800 m² of Guerlain-signature treatments at the edge of the park: an indoor pool beneath a glass roof, a hammam, a sauna and 14 bespoke treatment cabins.
  • Promenade en barque sur le Grand Canal — Boat hire on the Grand Canal in the palace grounds (up to 4 people), open until 15 November: a royal outing at the heart of the Estate, official ticketing.
