Europe's most prestigious horse-racing event: ParisLongchamp in festive attire, prestige hospitality. The flagship race of world racing, grandstands and VIP boxes bringing together elegance and the international jet-set.

Line-up : Owners, international jet-set, racing personalities

The turnkey stay

Two days on the edge of the Bois de Boulogne: a private box facing the ParisLongchamp track, Michelin-starred tables beneath the trees and nights in a palace hotel in the sixteenth arrondissement.

Where to stay

  • Saint James Paris — A Relais & Châteaux château-hotel just steps from the Bois de Boulogne: fifty rooms, a 400 m² Guerlain spa and a pool beneath a glass roof.
  • Shangri-La Paris — A palace hotel in the 16th arrondissement, set in the private mansion of Prince Roland Bonaparte, with views over the Seine and the Michelin-starred Shang Palace restaurant.

Where to dine

  • Le Pré Catelan — A Napoleon III pavilion in the heart of the Bois de Boulogne, three Michelin stars, Frédéric Anton's cuisine since 1997.
  • La Grande Cascade — A Belle Époque rotunda beneath a glass roof on the Allée de Longchamp, Michelin-starred since 1965: Frédéric Robert's table and its terrace.

What to experience

  • Hospitalités ParisLongchamp par France Galop — Private boxes, lounges and seated lunches offered by France Galop for both race days, right beside the track.
  • Yachts de Paris — Private charter of a yacht on the Seine, dinner on board and a cruise past the monuments, with six boats from the most intimate to the most spacious.
