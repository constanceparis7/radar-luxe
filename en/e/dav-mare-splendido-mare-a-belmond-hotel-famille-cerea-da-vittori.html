DaV Mare, Splendido Mare, A Belmond Hotel (Cerea Family / Da Vittorio)

Open for the summer 2026 season (July-August) · Splendido Mare, A Belmond Hotel, Via Roma 2, sur la Piazzetta et le port de Portofino · Other
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 80/100

Portofino's chic fish trattoria, born of the union between the Splendido Mare (Belmond) and the Cerea family of Da Vittorio. Terrace on the legendary Piazzetta: a society stage and meeting point for the people who matter on the harbour (a palace venue, not merely a gastronomic table).

Line-up : The Cerea family (Da Vittorio); the harbour's jet-set clientele

Access

Booking via davmare.com. The signature table of the Cerea family (Da Vittorio, three Michelin stars) on the Piazzetta, with harbour views. Society atmosphere and people-watching at the heart of Portofino; few tables, so book well ahead.

The turnkey stay

After the Cereas' table on the legendary Piazzetta, the dolce vita continues at the Splendido, monastery-turned-palace, a Michelin star and a sunset outing by Riva.

Where to stay

  • Splendido Mare, A Belmond Hotel — The home of DaV Mare: an intimate Belmond palace hotel on the Piazzetta and the harbour, steps from the Cereas' table.
  • Belmond Hotel Splendido — A 16th-century former monastery overlooking the bay, heated seawater pool and gardens; the legendary address of the Riviera.

Where to dine

  • Cracco Portofino — One Michelin star for 2026, chef Mattia Pecis (Young Chef Award); refined Ligurian cuisine facing the harbour and the Piazzetta.
  • Ristorante Puny — A cult institution on the Piazzetta for generations: pappardelle al Portofino and a society parade, booking essential.

What to experience

  • Croisiere privee en Riva au couchant — Riva Rivarama with skipper to San Fruttuoso and the Golfo Paradiso, aperitif at sunset.
  • Spa & piscine d'eau de mer du Splendido — The Wellness Centre of the Belmond Splendido: sea-view treatments and a heated pool above the bay of Portofino.
